1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the independent operator of damagerestorer.com ("we", "us" or "our") may collect, use and share information when you visit this website or contact the telephone number shown on it. The website provides general information about property damage restoration and operates for lead-generation and referral purposes. It does not necessarily provide restoration services itself.

By using the website or choosing to telephone the displayed number, you should understand the information practices described below. This policy should be read alongside the actual practices of the website operator and any independent service provider you choose to contact.

2. Information We Collect

The information involved will depend on how you use the website and telephone service. It may include, where applicable:

  • Information you provide voluntarily: details you choose to give when making an enquiry or discussing a potential service.
  • Telephone information: your telephone number, caller identification information and the date, time or duration of a call, where these are made available by the telephone service.
  • Information in telephone enquiries: details you provide about a property, damage, location, contact preferences or the assistance you are seeking.
  • Basic technical information: limited information that may be generated when a browser visits a website, such as the IP address, browser type, device information and pages requested, depending on the hosting and technical services in use.

This page does not contain an enquiry form. We do not state that analytics, advertising cookies or other tracking technologies are in use. The website operator should check the live hosting and telephone arrangements to confirm whether any additional information is collected.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, help connect callers with relevant independent service providers, facilitate a service discussion or consultation requested by a caller, maintain and improve the website and the quality of enquiries, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website is independently operated for informational and lead-generation purposes. It is not the restoration contractor and does not necessarily carry out, supervise or guarantee any restoration work.

If you telephone the number displayed on this website, your call may be connected or referred to an independent third-party business that provides the services you are asking about. Information you share may be passed to that provider so that it can respond to your enquiry. The website operator may receive compensation for generating or referring qualifying enquiries.

Any independent provider you are referred to is a separate business. It is responsible for its own services, decisions and privacy practices. Before proceeding, you should review the provider's terms and privacy information and ask it how it will use your details. Calling this website's number does not, by itself, mean that you have agreed to receive unrelated marketing communications.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone calls may be routed or measured using third-party telecommunications services. Depending on the arrangements in place, those services may process call details such as caller number, call time, duration or routing information. The operator must confirm which call-handling and tracking features are actually enabled.

This policy does not assert that calls are recorded. If call recording is used, callers must receive any notice and provide any consent required by applicable law before recording takes place. The operator should ensure that the live telephone process and any recording arrangements reflect this requirement.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Cookies are small files that a website may store on a visitor's device. The current page design does not visibly provide a cookie banner or identify an analytics provider. We do not claim that non-essential cookies, analytics tools or advertising pixels are used. Hosting or other technical services may use essential technologies to deliver and secure the website; the operator should verify the live configuration and update this policy if it uses additional cookies or analytics.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with an independent service provider when this is needed to handle or refer your enquiry, and with technical or telecommunications providers involved in hosting, maintaining or operating the website and telephone service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or others.

We do not describe every possible recipient here because the providers actually used depend on the website's hosting, telephone and referral arrangements. The operator should keep those arrangements under review and ensure this policy accurately reflects them.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No website, telephone system or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including handling an enquiry, maintaining appropriate business records and meeting legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the systems and providers used.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to personal information, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, or request its deletion. You may also have other rights under local privacy law. These rights are subject to applicable conditions and exceptions.

To make a privacy request, contact the website operator using its published contact details. The operator's legal name and a suitable privacy contact method should be added to this page before publication so that visitors have a clear way to submit requests.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website may direct you to, or connect you with, independent businesses and external websites. Those businesses and websites operate separately and may have their own privacy policies, terms and data-handling practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties. Please review their information before providing them with personal details.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website, its services or its information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the updated date shown near the top. You should review this page from time to time.

Website operator completion required: Before publication, add the operator's legal name and a working privacy contact method, and verify the actual call-routing, call-tracking, recording, hosting, cookie and analytics arrangements. This policy must be checked against the practices that are really in place; publishing it alone does not establish legal compliance.
