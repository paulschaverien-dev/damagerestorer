About damage restoration

Getting a property back to a safe, usable condition

Damage to a home or business can be disruptive and stressful. Water, fire, smoke, mold and severe weather can affect the structure, contents and indoor environment of a property.

Restoration work generally begins with understanding the extent of the damage, making the area safe, and planning the appropriate clean-up, drying, removal or repair work. The right approach depends on the cause and severity of the damage.

Areas of restoration

Damage Restoration Services

Different types of property damage require different assessment and restoration methods.

01

Water Damage

Water removal, structural drying and dehumidification may be needed after leaks, plumbing failures, flooding or other water incidents.

02

Fire & Smoke Damage

Fire restoration can involve debris removal, cleaning of smoke and soot residues, odour treatment and repair of affected areas.

03

Mold Remediation

Addressing moisture sources and dealing appropriately with affected materials are important parts of mold remediation.

04

Storm Damage

Storm-related work may include assessing roof or structural damage, clearing debris and arranging necessary repairs.

05

Property Clean-up

Cleaning and removal of damaged materials can help prepare affected spaces for repair and reinstatement.

06

Emergency Planning

Promptly identifying immediate risks and taking sensible steps to limit further damage can make a difference after an incident.

A considered approach

What matters during restoration

Every property and incident is different. A careful, clearly explained process helps owners understand the work involved.

Act with care

Safety comes first. Avoid entering areas that may be structurally unsafe or affected by electrical, smoke or contamination hazards.

Understand the damage

A proper assessment helps identify visible damage, potential hidden issues and the work that may be required.

Keep communication clear

Owners should understand the proposed scope of work, expected stages and any decisions that need their approval.

Plan the reinstatement

Once clean-up and drying are complete where needed, damaged building elements can be repaired or replaced as appropriate.

The usual stages

A straightforward restoration process

Initial assessment

Identify the source and extent of the damage, consider immediate safety concerns and decide what needs attention first.

Mitigation and clean-up

Where appropriate, limit further damage, remove affected materials and carry out the necessary cleaning or drying.

Repair and recovery

Plan repairs and reinstatement to return the affected areas to a suitable condition for use.
